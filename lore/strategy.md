# Strategy — Long-Game Design Doctrine

Applies to game design work (Redwood Prison etc.) and to any long-horizon project.

## The five levers

1. **Economy** — resources must breathe: inflow, sinks, feedback loops.
2. **Pacing** — calm build-up → crisis → recovery → escalation.
3. **Replayability** — random events, rival factions, trait variety.
4. **Player fantasy** — every system should make the player feel like *the* warden.
5. **Verification** — a balance claim is a hypothesis until playtested.

## Antipatterns

- Infinite growth with no sink.
- One dominant strategy.
- Busywork systems that never create a story worth retelling.

## Long-game mindset

- Design the loop first, content second.
- Ask of every feature: "Does the player retell this later?"
- Prefer systems that generate their own variety over hand-authored content.
